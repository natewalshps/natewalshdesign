The Idea

Most portfolios animate everything and say nothing.

This one picks a single accent, a single typeface family, and three repeatable moves — instead of a different effect on every scroll. Restraint is the design decision, not the absence of one.

Northwind

A dark, systemized product interface — built to show restraint at scale rather than to impress on the first scroll.

Problem

Teams kept shipping one-off flourishes because there was no shared vocabulary for motion.

Every new feature invented its own transition, its own easing, its own idea of "polished." Nothing matched, and nothing was reusable.

Solution

Three moves, documented once, reused everywhere.

Reveal on arrival, shift on state change, transition through one shared template — codified as tokens and components so any designer on the team could apply them without reinventing the system.

Result

A product that feels considered, page to page, without a heavier build.

This panel is the demo: it's the same hero template as the homepage, the same reveal timing, and the same accent — just re-pointed at different content.
