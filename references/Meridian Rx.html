Challenge

The old portal grew feature by feature for a decade, with claims, formulary, and reporting each built by a different team at a different time. Administrators memorized workarounds instead of using the product.

Solution

We rebuilt the navigation around the six tasks administrators actually do — not the six systems behind them — and gave every claim a single, consistent status language across the product.

Outcome

The resulting pattern library now powers three additional Meridian products, and new administrators reach their first resolved claim without a training session.

Motion earns its place only when it explains a state change — not when it decorates one.

01 — Task‑based IA

Six tasks replaced six systems

The legacy nav mirrored the org chart of internal systems. We interviewed administrators, mapped what they were actually trying to get done, and rebuilt the top level around those tasks — cutting the average path to a resolved claim from seven clicks to three.

02 — Status language

One status system, everywhere

Approved, pending, and in‑review meant something different in every module. We defined one status system with fixed color, label, and motion rules, then used it identically in claims, prior auth, and reporting — so trust in the data didn't have to be rebuilt screen by screen.
