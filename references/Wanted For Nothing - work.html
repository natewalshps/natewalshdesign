Rent.com

Strategy Design Development

Project

  • Client: Rent
  • Type: Informational site
  • Year: 2022

Services

  • —Strategy

  • —UX/UI Design

Challenge

Redesign and rebuild Rent’s B2B website. The goal was to achieve a similar look and feel as the rebranded consumer site, meanwhile taking a different approach and highlighting the business benefits for another target audience. 

Solution

We suggested highlighting the industry-leading tech that Rent. offers, including their marketplace, social and search offerings, along with the most up-to-date content: webinars, reports, and blog. The scalable site is designed to be able to accommodate the ever-growing offerings, new features, and content that Rent. provides.

Result

The new site has proven both internally and externally to be a powerful B2B tool for introducing and communicating Rent.'s platform features. Elements of the site have since been used in corporate communications, as well as for internal presentations. With new launches and consistent updates to both functionality and content, solutions.rent.com is a masterclass in presenting B2B solutions in the home rental category.
