- Aim for 2–3 posts per week

- Mix of pillar types: 1 strategic/thought leadership + 1 project insight + 1 craft/process tip

| **Format** | **Best For** | **Frequency** |

| Story post (300–600 words) | Project lessons, career moments, turning points | Bi-weekly |
| Framework / list post | Sharing a mental model or process you use | Monthly |
